# Who is Sus? 🕵️‍♂️🔍

Welcome to **Who is Sus**, a feature-rich, browser-based social deduction game built with **p5.js** and **PeerJS**. Play singleplayer or connect with friends in multiplayer rooms to figure out who among you is the hidden killer!

---

## 🎭 Roles (17 Unique Roles)

* **Killer**: Eliminate targets without getting caught.
* **Guardian Angel**: Protect players from elimination.
* **Sheriff**: Investigate and neutralize threats.
* **Doctor**: Revive or heal fallen teammates.
* **Detective**: Gather clues and track player activities.
* **Jester**: Trick others into voting you out to win.
* **Innocent**: Survive and complete tasks.
* **Hunter**: Take someone down with you if eliminated.
* **Vampire**: Convert or drain targets.
* **Swapper**: Intercept and switch target outcomes.
* **Hacker**: Bypass systems and reveal information.
* **Altruist**: Sacrifice yourself to save an ally.
* **Witch**: Cast spells and manipulate players.
* **Warden**: Lock down and manage custody of suspects.
* **Executioner**: Force specific targets into elimination.
* **Arsonist**: Douse players and trigger massive ignitions.
* **Magic Killer**: Execute covert eliminations with magical abilities.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Engine**: [p5.js](https://p5js.org/) (Canvas rendering & UI)
* **Networking**: [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com/) (WebRTC peer-to-peer data channels)
* **Persistence**: HTML5 `localStorage` & encrypted local vaults
